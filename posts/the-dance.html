Alternate titles: Why does the slave ever love?, Companion piece to pier-i

Prologue: From the Start
When the music grows softer, one's shadow manifests a song. We go quiet.

drop dead
How often does this happen? It was desperation, not luck. What is the calculus here? A ragged, filthy, bitter beggar shows up and you offer up all the alms you've got. And all the alms your poor mother has hidden inside her Spanish boots of Spanish leather. Pisces and a Gemini. Have you grown to like the Amazon more than the center of the Iberian peninsula? How do you feel about the Hudson?

stupid song
Yours is the evening, yours is the stage. Yours are the children dying on the streets. You're a spark. You're that Russian swan. You're music. You're an opera. You are tulips. You are salmon and the plate it's served on. You are caffeine, you are sugar. You are sunlight, you are shade. You are duty, you are indulgence. You are Christ and the cross he died on. You're carbon. You're water.

less
I carry my past. Like dolphins carry their pasts. I see you through the lens of my past like a dolphin sees you through the lens of his past. I may never swim. You touch the waters and my stomach's in knots. You were Xavier and I was the beaches of Goa. My Alhambra. I'm used to hiding my clouds when it's sunny and I murder my muse. I take a gun to the carnival and I murder my muse. Maybe I will fix myself tomorrow.

begged
What is broken may never break. I rehearsed my moves every night and I begged. I bit my tongue to make my case and I begged. I could never learn how to dance, which is why I never became a dancer. I do not know Tchaikovsky like your Siberian swans. The pier sinks low every passing day, while I thank no one for a dance. The seagulls screech in pain and I thank no one for a dance. It's a statistical aberration that you just have eyes for me.

the cure
Your mother ran for flesh and money. You told me you would break the chain. You despise all that is on this side of the Hudson yet you tunnel through every day. Horses, Haciendas, Christmas, Collectibles, or should I leave you alone? I think I have found the antidote.

honeybee
God, I love the way you look at me.
